## Sponsor specifics that matter for BillboardOS

### Hyphenate (printed p. 13)
Four judging prompts: realistic messy data; would you hire these seats (bounded access, audit log, escalation); novelty; and the **moment** the system catches, recovers or defends a decision. They ask for a demo video opening on that moment, a live demo, a one-line statement of seats filled, and a clear breakdown of what is real versus simulated.

### Coinbase x402 (printed p. 11)
The official guide **links out** for detailed x402 eligibility. A proposed use is an x402-gated slot booking endpoint with payment verification before booking. **x402 is not a negotiation agent or invoice system**; the application owns agreement, inventory, bookkeeping and settlement records. [External track guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12TnPLguduV3gh1zY_TJ176XmVDz2RGW9/view?usp=sharing).

### CRS (printed pp. 12–13)
The guide requires real sandbox calls during the live demo, not a decorative mention of CRS. Sandbox/test identities: [calhacks.crscreditapi.com](https://calhacks.crscreditapi.com). The exact products/endpoints accessible to the team must be confirmed. Use **only supplied test identities**; do not equate weak credit signals with proof of fraud.

### CREAO (printed pp. 11–12)
Three tracks share one prize pool. For each, use the relevant evaluation page and demonstrate how the autonomous business actually runs. [CREAO Cal Hacks page](https://community.creao.ai/CalHacks2026?utm_source=calhacks13-2026&utm_medium=hackathon&utm_campaign=hackathons-oct-2026).

### Hardware and ecosystem
The guide also includes non-track hardware resources and workshops. See [competition overview](index.md). A camera/ESP32/display demo alone does **not** satisfy an API sponsor track.

## Source limitations

The guide contains links to several **separate rule documents**; their contents have not been verified in this catalog. Revisit each sponsor booth or latest starter pack before submission. Prices and prizes reflect the supplied guide, not a current award guarantee.
