# Competition guide: Cal Hacks 13.0

**Official source:** Uploaded `13.0 Hacker Guide.pdf` (27 PDF pages). Printed page numbers below refer to the guide's own pagination.

## High-priority rules

| Item | Official guide |
|---|---|
| Venue | Palace of Fine Arts, 3601 Lyon St, San Francisco, CA 94123 (printed p. 3) |
| Team size | 1–4 people (p. 7) |
| Work | Projects must be started **during the event** (p. 7). Pre-existing planning/notes should not be presented as pre-built project code. |
| Submission | Devpost by **Sunday, 9:00 AM** (p. 7) |
| Judging | In person **Sunday, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM**; team must stay at assigned table (pp. 7–8) |
| Pitch | Prepare about **2 minutes**, and demonstrate actual working functionality live (pp. 7–8) |
| Criteria | Impact, quality, technical complexity, creativity; judges value genuine effort and thought process (pp. 7–8) |
| Sponsor resources | Starter packs/criteria may be updated on the live site; prize information subject to change (p. 10) |

## General awards (printed pp. 9–10)

First Overall — MacBook Air 13-inch M5; Second Overall — Nintendo Switch 2; Third Overall — Google Intelligent Eyewear; Most Creative — Instax camera + film; Most Technically Complex — NuPhy Air60 HE; Best People-First — Apple Watch SE 3; Best Planet-First — America the Beautiful Parks Pass; Best Hardware — Bambu Lab A1 Mini; Best Beginner — AirPods 4; Best Solo — AirPods Pro 3; Hacker's Choice — Logitech MX Master 4.

## Hardware (printed pp. 23–24)

Rental booth lists Arduino Uno, Raspberry Pi/Pico, ESP32, MPU6050 and assorted sensor modules. MLH lab lists webcams and additional components. Hardware is limited and availability must be checked. The guide says **no soldering** in the venue. Five 3D printers with PLA are listed, limited to 3.5 hours per print. ASUS GX10s and Seeed reBot arms are also mentioned, with restricted availability / sign-up requirements.
