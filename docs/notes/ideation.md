# Original idea and brainstorm synthesis

**Provenance:** team-provided `pasted.txt` plus two whiteboard/handwritten JPEGs. **This page summarizes the written ideas**; it is not an audio transcription.

## Original written premise

Imagine an advertising agency operating digital billboards in **Times Square, San Francisco and Seattle**. Each billboard has an agent that understands available inventory, approximate audience reach and matching advertisers; the overall business runs with minimal staff.

### Six functions captured verbatim in meaning

1. Audience/location understanding using cameras and sensors plus contextual information.
2. Dynamic pricing based on reach estimates, demand, event schedules and time.
3. Tiered packages for startups and large advertisers.
4. Brief/document understanding: target users, budgets, target cities and ad creative.
5. Prospecting and communication: identify relevant businesses and send proposals, negotiate and follow up.
6. Booking/finance: invoicing, payments, scheduling, playback logs and revenue dashboard.

### Optional original extensions

- An advertiser's buying agent negotiates with a billboard seller agent against a budget and seller floor.
- **x402**-enabled booking endpoint processes machine payment **after agreement**; x402 does not itself negotiate.
- **CRS** may provide identity / risk data subject to actual test APIs. Weak credit is not equivalent to fraud.
- Physical demonstration could use one webcam and one monitor, one quote engine, brief upload and a booking dashboard.
